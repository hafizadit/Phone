Neuroscientist Robert Sapolsky says no. Every “choice” is the last domino in a chain you never controlled:

Months agoExperience rewired your neurons
Hours agoHormones tuned your mood
Seconds agoYour senses caught a cue
1 second agoNeurons fired
Now“I decided.”
So who deserves the credit — or the blame?
