# Story: Did you choose to read this? (Sapolsky, free will)

One Instagram story, 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Final image: `story.png`.

- `story.html` — design source
- `render.js` — export to PNG: `node render.js` (needs Playwright)

Concept: every "choice" is the last domino in a chain of causes (evolution → … →
neurons fired → "I decided."), then Sapolsky vs. his compatibilist critics.
The top ~220 px and bottom ~150 px are kept clear for Instagram's UI.
