# Story: You can't move the old dots (Sapolsky)

One Instagram story, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), series part 5 (hope), after `../past-you`,
`../rapor`, `../pencil-case`, `../tracing`. Final image: `story.png`.
Rebuild: `node build.js && node render.js`.

Visual: a "sambung titik" (connect-the-dots) page from a children's activity book.
Old dots 1–6 are already joined in grey ("gen", "masa kecil", "stres"). New
coloured dots 7–10 keep the line going but turn it upward ("buku", "teman baik",
"terapi", "story ini"), and a crayon is still drawing towards dot 10. Same crayon
language as the series, from `../shared/hand.js`.

## Content and accuracy
- Sapolsky (*Determined*) says people do change, but not through free will. They
  change because new input reaches them: experiences, other people, things they
  read. He uses reading something as an example of such a cause, which is why
  "story ini" is one of the new dots.
- The past stays fixed ("you can't move the old dots"). Change comes from what
  gets added, hence "That's how people change."
