# Story: Stop comparing yourself to a 3-level pencil case (Sapolsky)

One Instagram story, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), series part 3 (after `../past-you`
and `../rapor`). Final image: `story.png`. Rebuild: `node build.js && node render.js`.

Visual: a fancy 3-tier pencil case ("punya dia") with stickers on the lid and
pens poking out, next to a plastic kresek with one stubby pencil ("punya kamu",
"dipakai sampai pendek"). Same crayon language as the series, from
`../shared/hand.js` (`createHand` + `createKit`: hand lettering, rough outlines,
messy `scribbleFill` colouring over a solid base).

## Content and accuracy
- Sapolsky (*Determined*) argues nobody "deserves" their advantages or
  disadvantages: what people achieve depends on genes and environment they never
  chose. This story applies that to comparing yourself with others, hence
  "After Robert Sapolsky".
- The labels on the case are environmental head starts he discusses in general
  terms: education resources ("les privat"), a low-stress home ("rumah tenang"),
  parental time ("ortu ada waktu") and nutrition ("gizi cukup").
