# Story: Your parents were tracing too (Sapolsky)

One Instagram story, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), series part 4 (after `../past-you`,
`../rapor`, `../pencil-case`). Final image: `story.png`.
Rebuild: `node build.js && node render.js`.

Visual: three sheets laid down and to the right of each other. The bottom one is the
original drawing ("kakek-nenek"), then a see-through tracing-paper copy
("orang tua"), then the newest sheet ("kamu"), only half traced with the pencil
still on it. Each copy of the house is a little different. A handwritten note
in the corner: "memahami bukan berarti membenarkan". Same crayon language as the
series, from `../shared/hand.js`.

## Content and accuracy
- Sapolsky's argument covers everyone, so it also covers parents: how they raised
  their children was shaped by how they themselves were raised and the conditions
  they lived in. Parenting patterns passing between generations is well documented.
  This story applies his view, hence "After Robert Sapolsky".
- Sapolsky argues that nobody deserves blame, but he does not tell people to
  forgive. So the story asks for *understanding* and says outright that
  understanding is not justifying. It must not read as excusing abuse.
