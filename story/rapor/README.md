# Story: "Kurang rajin" was never the whole story (Sapolsky)

One Instagram story, 1080 × 1920 (9:16), part of the series after `../past-you`.
Final image: `story.png`. Rebuild: `node build.js && node render.js`.

Visual: a primary-school report card ("rapor") whose teacher's note says
"Kurang rajin. Sering melamun di kelas.", with sticky notes adding what it left out.
Everything in the drawing is crayon, like past-you: hand-lettered text, freehand
table, page and notes coloured in with crayon hatching over a solid base (so
nothing behind them shows through), no flat digital fills or drop shadows.
The hand-drawn toolkit and filters are shared: `../shared/hand.js`.

## Content and accuracy
- Sapolsky (*Determined*, *Behave*): self-control depends on the frontal cortex,
  which develops late (into the mid-20s) and is shaped by childhood environment and
  stress. So "lazy" is not a full explanation. This is an application of his view,
  hence "After Robert Sapolsky".
- Sticky notes are everyday examples of those factors: stress at home
  ("rumah lagi ribut"), lack of sleep, hunger, and a still-developing brain.
- The story avoids "willpower runs out" (ego depletion), which did not replicate well.
- Red grades are below a passing grade (KKM 65), as Indonesian teachers write them.
